Questions students ask us

Will you write my thesis or chapters?

No. Miss Adhyapika is a consultancy. You write your thesis; we guide the research, review your drafts, explain the analysis and edit your language. This keeps your degree safe and prepares you for the viva.

Is it allowed to take help from a consultant?

Guidance, statistical consulting and language editing are common and generally accepted, much like a tutor. Check your university's rules, and acknowledge the support you received if your institution requires it.

Which subjects do you cover?

Management, commerce, social sciences, education, psychology, social work, public health, nursing, life sciences and engineering. Share your topic and we'll match you with a consultant from your field.

How are sessions held?

Online on Google Meet or Zoom, in English or Hindi. Students in Pune can also book in-person sessions.

Will my data and draft stay private?

Yes. We sign a confidentiality agreement with every student and never share or reuse your work.

What if I'm not satisfied?

Each package includes revision rounds. If a milestone hasn't started, its advance is refundable under our refund policy.
